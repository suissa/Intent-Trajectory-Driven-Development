# Destination Skill

The Destination Skill is the operational form of the T-DD Destiny stage.

## Source

- [T-DD README](../README.md)
- [T-DD semantic SRFC](../docs/SRFCs/SRFC-T-DD-Semantic-Model.md)
- [Define Destination Atomic Skill](atomic/define-destination-SKILL.md)

## When to use

Use before defining implementation, Intent execution or Trajectory details when the business outcome is not explicit.

## Procedure

1. State the business outcome.
2. Define what completion means.
3. Define mandatory completion evidence.
4. Reject technology-specific outcomes such as "create an API" as the destination.
5. Hand the Destination to the Intent Skill.

## Output

A business destination with explicit completion conditions.
